Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 24, 2026

Rootly ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the Rootly: Hair Growth Tracker mobile application.

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: When you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your name and email address.
  • Profile data: Information you provide during onboarding such as your gender, birth year, hair goals, and hair care routine.
  • Progress photos: Photos you choose to upload to track your hair growth journey. These are stored securely and are only accessible by you.
  • Usage data: Daily logs, routine task completions, XP, and level progress within the app.
  • Purchase information: Subscription status and transaction history processed through Apple and RevenueCat.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and personalize the Rootly experience
  • To track your hair growth progress over time
  • To manage your subscription and provide access to Rootly Pro features
  • To send you reminders and notifications you have opted into
  • To improve the app and fix bugs

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a trusted cloud database provider. Progress photos are stored in a private, access-controlled storage bucket. We use industry-standard security practices to protect your information from unauthorized access.

Third-Party Services

Rootly uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase - Database and file storage (Privacy Policy)
  • RevenueCat - Subscription management (Privacy Policy)
  • Apple Sign In - Authentication (Privacy Policy)
  • Google Sign In - Authentication (Privacy Policy)

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share your information with third parties except as necessary to operate the app (such as the services listed above) or as required by law.

Progress Photos

Photos you upload are stored privately and are only visible to you. We do not use your photos for any purpose other than displaying them within your personal account in the app.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Opt out of notifications at any time through your device settings

To request account deletion or data export, please contact us at customersupport.rootly@gmail.com.

Children's Privacy

Rootly is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page.
